# The training program

Mat Strength ships with a 4-day strength program designed for someone who trains BJJ about 5 times a week and gets their conditioning on the mats, not in the gym.

## Why 4 days, and why two of them are light

Most BJJ strength coaches recommend 2 to 3 lifting days for athletes who train BJJ often, and some say 2 is enough at 5+ mat sessions a week. Four days only works if two of them are genuinely easy. The program therefore uses two hard days built around heavy compound lifts and power work, and two light days of accessory, prehab, grip and trunk work.

The app counts CrossFit as a training day. If you do a CrossFit class, it should replace a light lifting day, not add to it.

## Weekly schedule guidelines

- Lift before BJJ when you do both on the same day, with a meal and a couple of hours in between.
- Put hard lifting days on drilling days and light lifting days on hard sparring days.
- Don't train the same muscles on back-to-back days.
- Take at least one full day off each week.

## The four days

**Day 1, hard: lower and pull.** Box or broad jump 3 × 3–5, front squat 3–4 × 3–5, Romanian deadlift 3 × 6–8, barbell row 3–4 × 6–8, farmer's walk 3 × 30 m, neck isometrics 3 × 10 s each side.

**Day 2, light: upper.** Incline dumbbell press 3 × 8–12, pull-ups 3 × max minus 2, rear delt raise or face pull 3 × 15, dumbbell curl and triceps extension 2–3 × 10–12, hanging knee raise 3 × 10–15.

**Day 3, hard: upper and hinge.** Med ball chest throw 3 × 3–5, bench press 3–4 × 3–5, deadlift 3 × 2–5, chin-ups 3 × 5–8, landmine rotation 3 × 5 per side, sandbag bear-hug carry 3 × 30 m.

**Day 4, light: lower and prehab.** Bulgarian split squat 3 × 8 per leg, hamstring curl 3 × 10–12, isometric back extension 3 × 30–45 s, side plank 3 × 30 s per side, grip work 3 sets.

Explosive work comes first in each session, then heavy compound lifts, then accessories: fast to slow, complex to simple.

## Intensity and blocks

Training runs in 7-week blocks followed by a deload week.

| Week | Stop each set about |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | 3 reps short of failure |
| 3–5 | 2 reps short |
| 6–7 | 1 rep short |
| 8 (deload) | about 2/3 of the sets, 20% less weight |

Add weight or a rep whenever you hit the top of a rep range on every set.

## Strength targets

Targets are set relative to bodyweight, because once bodyweight is accounted for, elite and non-elite BJJ athletes don't differ much in raw strength.

| Lift | Target |
|---|---|
| Bench press | 1.3–1.5 × bodyweight |
| Squat | 1.5–2.0 × bodyweight |
| Deadlift | 2.0–2.5 × bodyweight |

Once you're in range, chasing bigger numbers matters less than power, grip, and upper-body and trunk endurance. The app compares these targets with your front squat, which typically runs lower than a back squat, so treat that bar as conservative.
